# Roadmap

*Draft — seeded from [background.md](background.md)'s "Looking ahead" section;
needs your own pass before this is really final, same as that page.*

Posetrak now largely achieves the goals the project started with: capturing
pair and multi-person aikido practice with equipment that's affordable for a
serious hobbyist, and it's been used successfully for plenty of activities
beyond aikido too. It's still very much a tool built for one person's needs —
polishing it into something more than that is ongoing.

## What's next

- Keep improving the solver and usability.
- Marker-based motion capture — the immediate use case is better tracking of
  props like weapons.
- Animal motion capture, eventually.

## What's deliberately not a priority

- **Single-camera capture.** For close-contact, multi-person scenes like
  aikido, single-camera capture isn't feasible until AI models have enough
  training material on exactly that kind of scene — which is, after all, the
  whole reason Posetrak exists as a multi-camera project.
- **Face mocap.** Not a current focus.
